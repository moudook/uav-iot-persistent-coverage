ITEM 1  --  R_cov = 15 m AGAINST THE K-COVERAGE CONDITION
================================================================================
  LOCATION : PDF page 6, TABLE IV, row L472
             coverage condition stated at L268, Eq. (8) at L255

  AS PRINTED
    L472   R_cov = 15 m
    L471   Omega = 100 x 100 m^2   ->  |Omega| = 10,000 m^2
    L470   M = 20
    L468   N = 5
    L474   K = 2

  REPORT'S OWN CONDITION
    L268   "The primary service condition is n_d(t) >= K for all d
            whenever the geometry and available fleet make it feasible."

  COMPUTED
    L255   n_d(t) = sum over i of x_id(t), so sum over all d of n_d
           counts sensor-UAV incidence pairs; K-coverage of all M sensors
           requires at least K*M = 40 such pairs.
    A UAV at position p covers sensors within a disk of area pi*R_cov^2.
    With M sensors uniform in area A, expected sensors in one disk is
    (M/A) * pi * R_cov^2. Summing over N UAVs:

      expected pairs = N * (M/A) * pi * R_cov^2
                     = 5 * (20/10,000) * pi * 225
                     = 5 * 0.002 * 706.858
                     = 7.069

      required pairs = K*M = 40
      ratio          = 7.069 / 40 = 0.1767

    Equivalently, in area units:
      N * pi * R_cov^2 = 3,534.29 m^2
      K * |Omega|      = 20,000.00 m^2

    Solving N * pi * R_cov^2 = K * |Omega| for the radius:
      R_cov = sqrt(K |Omega| / (pi N))
            = sqrt(2 * 10,000 / (pi * 5))
            = sqrt(1273.2395) = 35.68 m
    With congruent-disk packing density delta = pi/(2 sqrt 3) = 0.90690:
      R_cov = sqrt(K |Omega| / (delta pi N)) = 37.47 m

  The ratio 0.1767 is below 1, and the corresponding K_max = floor(0.1767)
  = 0. The condition at L268 as printed is not met for any policy.

  GRID CHECK. For 5 x 4 sensors over 100 x 100 m, spacing is 20.0 m by
  25.0 m. A 15 m disk centred at a grid point contains 1 sensor. With
  N = 5 the swarm supplies 5 pairs against 40 required. Same result for
  4 x 5 and for 4 x 4.

  REPRODUCE
    python Project1/research/feasibility_check.py
