SECTION 2  --  ARITHMETIC ON THE TABLE IV VALUES
================================================================================
  Reproduce: python Project1/research/feasibility_check.py

2.1  AREA COMPARISON AT K = 2

     supplied   N * pi * R_cov^2  = 5 * pi * 15^2   =  3,534.29 m^2
     required   K * |Omega|                            = 20,000.00 m^2
     ratio                                            =  0.1767

     K_max = floor(0.1767) = 0

     The ratio 0.1767 is the fraction of the required coverage area that
     N disks of radius R_cov can supply. A value below 1 means the area
     condition fails; K_max = 0 means the K = 1 condition also fails.

2.2  SOLVED FOR R_cov

     N * pi * R_cov^2 = K * |Omega|
     R_cov = sqrt(K |Omega| / (pi N))
     R_cov = sqrt(2 * 10,000 / (pi * 5))  =  sqrt(1273.2395)  =  35.68 m

     Packing-corrected, with congruent-disk packing density
     delta = pi / (2 sqrt 3) = 0.90690:
     R_cov = sqrt(K |Omega| / (delta pi N))
     R_cov = sqrt(20,000 / (0.90690 * pi * 5))  =  sqrt(1403.86)  =  37.47 m

     Table IV prints 15 m.

2.3  UNIFORM-GRID CHECK

     For sensors on a uniform grid with spacing s, a disk of radius
     R_cov centred at a grid point contains the sensors within
     2 R_cov. With 5 x 4 sensors over 100 x 100 m, spacing is
     20.0 m (horizontal) and 25.0 m (vertical).

       grid   spacing        max sensors in one 15 m disk
       5 x 4  20.0 x 25.0    1
       4 x 5  25.0 x 20.0    1
       4 x 4  25.0 x 25.0    1

     With 1 sensor per UAV, N = 5 gives 5 coverage slots. K*M = 2*20 = 40
     are required.

2.4  ENERGY, TABLE IV VALUES

     Hover power incl. comm  = 50 + 10 = 60 W
       endurance = 30,000 / 60 = 500.0 s = 8.33 min
     Move power incl. comm   = 75 + 10 = 85 W
       endurance = 30,000 / 85 = 352.9 s = 5.88 min
     Charge 0.01 per step at dt = 1 s
       full 0->1 charge = 1 / 0.01 = 100 s = 1.67 min
       charge/flight ratio = 100 / 352.9 = 0.283

     Return threshold. Eq. (15) at .tex L337-L339 defines
     E_i^req = E_i^pad + E_i^rejoin + E^reserve.
     Longest transit in region = diagonal = sqrt(2) * 100 = 141.42 m
       at v_max = 5 m/s -> 28.28 s -> 85 W * 28.28 s = 2,404 J
     Rejoin to region edge    = 100 m
       at v_max = 5 m/s -> 20.0 s -> 85 W * 20.0 s = 1,700 J
     Reserve at 10% of B_max = 3,000 J
     E^req = 2,404 + 1,700 + 3,000 = 7,104 J = 23.7% of B_max
     So the condition E_i <= E_i^req at L341 triggers at b_i <= 0.763.

2.5  TIME-STEP RESOLUTION

     Control steps across the coverage diameter = 2 R_cov / (v_max * dt)
       with Table IV values:  2*15 / (5*1) = 6.0 steps

2.6  COMMUNICATION-GRAPH DENSITY

     Expected edges for N points uniform in area A with radius R:
       E[|E|] = C(N,2) * pi R^2 / A
     With R_comm = 40, N = 5, A = 10,000:
       pi*40^2/10,000 = 0.5027
       E[|E|] = 10 * 0.5027 = 5.03
     A connected graph on 5 vertices requires at least 4 edges.

2.7  ACTION SPACE, Eq. (4) at .tex L216-L218

     A_i = {Hover, +x, -x, +y, -y, Charge},  |A_i| = 6
       6^4  =      1,296
       6^6  =     46,656
       6^10 =  60,466,176
       6^20 = 3,656,158,440,062,976

     Intro at L66 states 6 actions and 6^N. This is consistent with
     Eq. (4). No discrepancy.

SECTION 5  --  OBJECTIVE SIGN CONVENTION
================================================================================
  Eq. (25) at .tex L408-L410
    J_out(pi) = E_pi[ sum_t gamma^t U_t ]
  Sec. III-L text at L407 states the primary objective as minimising
  service loss, and the paper writes "The control policy therefore
  prioritizes minimizing long-horizon service loss".

  Eq. (26) at L415-L420
    min_pi  J_out + lambda_E J_E + lambda_D J_D + lambda_W J_W
                 + lambda_R J_R + lambda_C J_C

  Eq. (28) at L425-L430
    r_t = -( lambda_O U_t + lambda_E E_t + lambda_D D_t
             + lambda_W W_t + lambda_R R_t + lambda_C Crit_t )

  Eq. (2) at L169-L171
    max_pi  E_pi[ sum_t gamma^t R_t ]

  Eq. (2) maximises. Eq. (26) minimises. Eq. (28) negates the weighted
  cost. No sentence in the document states the relationship between the
  minimisation form of Eq. (26) and the maximisation form of Eq. (2).
  The three equations are mutually consistent up to sign.
