\begin{tcolorbox}[title={\bfseries The one thing you must know}]
The report's parameter table currently makes the stated objective
\emph{physically unachievable}.

With $|\Omega|=100\times100$~m$^2$, $M=20$ sensors, $N=5$ UAVs, $K=2$ and
$R_{\mathrm{cov}}=15$~m, the fleet supplies
\[
  N\pi R_{\mathrm{cov}}^2 = 3\,534\ \text{m}^2
\]
of coverage against a requirement of
\[
  KA = 2\times 10\,000 = 20\,000\ \text{m}^2 ,
\]
only \(17.7\%\) of what is needed. The necessary condition is
\[
  R_{\mathrm{cov}} \;\ge\; \sqrt{\frac{KA}{\pi N}} \;=\; 35.68\ \text{m},
\]
and the table says \(15\) m.

\textbf{Fix:} $\Omega = 80\times80$ m, $R_{\mathrm{cov}}=35$ m,
$R_{\mathrm{comm}}=45$ m, $B^{\mathrm{max}}=60$ kJ, charge $0.001$/step.
Surplus ratio $\sigma = 1.50$, dropping to $1.20$ after a failure.

Two further must-fixes: the articulation indicator $a_i$ collides with the
action $a_i$ (rename to $\kappa_i$), and reference \texttt{chen2025} is
attributed to the wrong author (Zhuang Chen, not Chen).
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}
