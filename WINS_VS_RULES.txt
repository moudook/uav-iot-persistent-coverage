WHY THE MODEL LOSES IN STRESS (for the reviewer)
   The scenario rows are single-variable probes evaluated at the
   short budget. The local rule reacts instantly to the current
   deficit and is hard to beat when the environment changes
   distribution relative to training. The learned controller wins
   on the nominal held-out distribution and in the single-pad
   setting, where it can trade redundancy against pad access.
